RESEARCH & CONTRIBUTIONS

Publication

IEEE ISCAIE 2025 · Penang, Malaysia

AgriDryTech: Development of an Automated System for Rice Drying and Storage

IEEE Published Scopus-Indexed Research Paper

AgriDryTech is an IEEE-published research project that focuses on the development of an automated rice drying and storage system designed to help Filipino farmers address challenges caused by unpredictable weather conditions and pest contamination.

The system utilizes controlled temperature and humidity to improve the efficiency and reliability of rice drying and storage.

The project was evaluated using ISO 9126 quality standards, achieving high ratings in functionality, reliability, usability, and efficiency.

The research was presented at the 2025 IEEE 15th Symposium on Computer Applications & Industrial Electronics (ISCAIE) in Penang, Malaysia. The publication is Scopus-indexed.
